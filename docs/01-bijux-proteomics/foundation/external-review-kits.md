# External Review Kits

The external review kits are the shortest outsider route through the current
flagship proof boundary.

Each kit combines:

- one outsider packet
- one independent rerun dossier
- the flagship benchmark package
- the companion challenge package
- the benchmark-backed recommendation surface
- the requested-versus-observed outcome dossier

## What A Kit Refuses To Hide

- bounded comparator posture
- bounded lab consequence posture
- remaining vendor or portability limits
- the fact that repository-owned rerun lanes are still narrower than broad
  third-party reproduction

## Reader Order

Open the flagship package first, then the companion rerun package, then the
outsider packet, then the remaining limits. The order matters because a trust
sentence is only worth reading after the underlying challenge surfaces are open.
